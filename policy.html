Privacy Policy

Pupo Padi Ride Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pupo Padi Ride collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when passengers and riders use our mobile application and related services.

Pupo Padi Ride is a ride connection technology platform that connects passengers with independent riders. We collect only the information required to operate the platform safely, process rides, manage wallets, support users, and comply with applicable requirements.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account type, such as passenger or rider
  • Profile information
  • Ride request details
  • Pickup and destination information
  • Location data required for ride matching and ride monitoring
  • Wallet transactions and payment-related records
  • Rider verification details
  • Vehicle details, including plate number where applicable
  • Communication and support records
  • Device and usage information needed for security and app improvement

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To connect passengers with available riders
  • To process ride requests and ride history
  • To support location-based ride features
  • To manage wallet balances and ride payments
  • To verify riders before activation
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve app performance, safety, and user experience
  • To prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activities
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, audit, and security obligations

3. Location Data

Pupo Padi Ride uses location data to connect passengers with riders, show pickup and destination points, support ride monitoring, and improve ride coordination.

Location data may be collected when using ride-related features. Some ride features may not function properly if location permission is denied.

4. Wallet and Payment Records

Ride payments are processed through wallet balances within the platform. Wallet activity, ride charges, completed ride records, and payment-related records may be stored for reconciliation, audit, dispute resolution, security, and financial record purposes.

5. Rider Verification

Riders are required to complete physical verification before becoming active on the platform. Admins may verify rider identity and vehicle information before allowing a rider to accept rides.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell user personal information. However, necessary information may be shared in the following situations:

  • Between passengers and riders for ride coordination
  • With support or admin personnel for verification and issue resolution
  • With service providers that help operate the platform
  • Where required by law, regulation, investigation, or lawful request
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, security threats, or platform misuse

7. Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no digital platform can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for keeping their login access and devices secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain financial records, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and support platform security.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by visiting:

https://pupopadi.com.ng/delete-account

Some information may be retained where required for legal, financial, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or regulatory purposes.

10. User Rights

Users may request to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account
  • Withdraw certain permissions, such as location permission
  • Contact support regarding privacy concerns

11. Children’s Privacy

Pupo Padi Ride is not intended for children. Users must be old enough to lawfully use ride-hailing or transport-related services in their location. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date where necessary.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or support, contact us:

Email: support@pupopadi.com.ng
Website: https://pupopadi.com.ng

This Privacy Policy applies to users of Pupo Padi Ride mobile application and related services.
