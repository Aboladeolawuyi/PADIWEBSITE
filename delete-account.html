Account & Data Deletion Request

Delete Your Pupo Padi Ride Account

At Pupo Padi Ride, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you would like to delete your account and associated data from our platform, please follow the instructions below.

Account deletion request email:
support@pupopadi.com.ng

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Pupo Padi Ride account, send an email to support@pupopadi.com.ng with the subject: Account Deletion Request.

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number
  • Your account type: Passenger or Rider

What Data Will Be Deleted

Once your request is received and your identity is verified, we will delete your account and associated personal data, including your profile information, contact details, and account records where applicable.

Data We May Retain

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial records, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Deletion Timeline

Account deletion requests are usually processed within 30 days after successful verification of the account owner.

Need Help?

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact us at support@pupopadi.com.ng.
