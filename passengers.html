Simple Booking

How to Book a Ride

Booking a ride on Pupopadi is quick and easy. Here's how it works.

1

Set Your Route

Open the app and enter where you're going. You'll see an estimated fare instantly.

2

Enter Your Price

Suggest a fair price for your trip. The app generates an OTP code for the driver.

3

Match & Ride

A nearby driver accepts your price, picks you up, and you enjoy the ride.

Secure Payments

Wallet & Payment

Fund your Pupopadi wallet and pay for rides seamlessly. No need to carry cash or fumble with cards at the end of every trip.

Easy Funding

Top up your wallet via bank transfer, card, or mobile money. Funds reflect instantly.

Secure Transactions

All payments are encrypted end-to-end. Your financial data is never stored on our servers.

Instant Withdrawal
