About Pupopadi Ride

We're building the future of transportation — one ride at a time. Accessible, affordable, and built for everyone.

Our Story

Born from a Simple Idea

Pupopadi Ride was founded with a clear mission: to make transportation work better for everyone. We saw a gap in the market — rides that were either too expensive, too unreliable, or too complicated to book.

So we built something different. A platform where passengers can negotiate fair prices directly with riders, where earnings are transparent, and where safety isn't an afterthought.

Today, Pupopadi connects passengers with trusted riders across the city. And we're just getting started.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and reliable transportation for everyone. We believe getting from point A to B shouldn't be a luxury — it should be a right. By empowering riders and giving passengers choice, we're building a fairer mobility ecosystem.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted ride-hailing platform in Africa. A platform where technology serves people, not the other way around. Where every trip creates value for both passenger and rider. Where transportation is seamless, safe, and sustainable.
